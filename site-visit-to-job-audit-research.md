# Site Visit to Job Audit — External Research Notes

## Lead Management

Salesforce describes small-business lead management as tracking a prospect from initial interest to becoming a paying customer. Its practical stages are identification, qualification, nurturing, and conversion. The guidance emphasizes centralizing lead data, defining qualification criteria, personalizing follow-up from lead history, and tracking interactions and conversion metrics. This supports maintaining a single linked record across the request, lead, quote, signed acceptance, deposit, and job stages rather than relying on disconnected indicators.

Source: [Salesforce — Lead Management For Small Businesses and Startups: Quick Guide](https://www.salesforce.com/blog/small-business/lead-management-for-startups/)

## Quote Approval Controls

HubSpot’s current quote-approval documentation identifies total discount, quote amount, terms, acceptance method, electronic signature, and payment enablement as distinct approval factors. It also illustrates routing different approval conditions separately. This supports keeping Phase 1 acceptance and payment distinct from optional future phases, retaining clear acceptance scope, and preserving an audit record of the customer’s acceptance method.

Source: [HubSpot — Quote approval use cases and commonly used properties](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/quotes/quote-approval-use-cases-and-commonly-used-properties)
