Sources: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/consumer-reviews-testimonials-rule-questions-answers and https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials
