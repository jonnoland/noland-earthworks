# Noland Earthworks Privacy Notice — Draft for Legal Review

**Status:** Draft based on the application’s current collection and service providers. This document is not legal advice and must be reviewed and approved by Tennessee-appropriate counsel before replacing the public privacy notice.

## Information We Collect

Noland Earthworks collects information that a visitor provides when requesting a site visit, contacting the business, using the chat feature, paying an invoice, or subscribing to updates. Depending on the interaction, this may include name, phone number, email address, property address or county, project details, preferred contact method, timing preferences, communications, photos or documents voluntarily supplied for a project, and payment-status information.

The business may also collect limited device and website-use information through analytics and cookies. Payment card information is processed by Stripe and is not intended to be stored in the Noland Earthworks operations system.

## How We Use Information

Information is used to respond to requests, determine whether the work is a fit, arrange a site visit, prepare and deliver a proposal, schedule and complete work, invoice and collect payment, provide service or weather updates, maintain business records, protect the website and operations system, and improve website and service operations.

The business may use AI-assisted tools to organize inquiry details, draft internal summaries or customer communications, prepare preliminary operational material, and help route work. AI output is reviewed by the owner before it is used to make a customer commitment. The business does not rely on AI alone to set final price, confirm scope, schedule work, approve a discount, or make a decision about a customer.

## Service Providers and Storage

The current website and operations stack uses service providers for hosting and storage, transactional email, SMS or phone communications, payment processing, mapping, website analytics, and AI-assisted operational drafting. These may include Manus-hosted infrastructure and storage, Resend, Twilio, Stripe, Google Maps and Analytics, and AI service infrastructure used through the application. Information is shared with a provider only as needed for the applicable function, subject to the provider’s own terms and privacy practices.

Noland Earthworks uses a native operations system as its day-to-day record for clients, leads, quotes, jobs, invoices, deposits, and payment status. Jobber is not the default system of record and is used only if the owner adopts it for a defined operational need.

## SMS and Email

If a visitor provides a phone number or email address in connection with a project, Noland Earthworks may contact that visitor by the requested method about the inquiry, site visit, proposal, scheduling, service updates, invoices, and payment. Message frequency varies by project. Message and data rates may apply. For SMS, recipients may reply **STOP** to opt out and **HELP** for help. Operational communications may be limited after opt-out where SMS is the requested contact method; alternate contact methods remain available.

Marketing messages, if used, must be subject to the consent and unsubscribe requirements applicable to the recipient and communication channel. This draft does not authorize any unreviewed marketing workflow.

## Retention, Access, and Requests

Noland Earthworks retains project and communication information for as long as reasonably needed to operate the business, resolve disputes, meet recordkeeping obligations, and enforce agreements. A person may request access, correction, or deletion of applicable personal information by contacting **quotes@nolandearthworks.com** or **(615) 406-4819**. Requests are subject to identity verification, legal requirements, and records the business is required to retain.
