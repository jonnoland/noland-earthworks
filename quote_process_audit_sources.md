- **Request a Site Visit (working URL):** https://nolandearthworks.com/quote

  - Step 1 requires name, phone, and email.

## External Lead-Response Research

- **LeanData, April 8, 2026:** https://www.leandata.com/blog/lead-response-time/
  - The source recommends separating lead-processing delay from representative response delay, tracking median and P90 response time in addition to averages, and assigning explicit response targets by lead type.
  - The source cites Harvard Business Review research on first-hour response and frames follow-up speed as a routing and ownership issue rather than a coaching issue alone.

- **InsideSales Lead Response Study 2021:** https://www.insidesales.com/response-time-matters/
  - The source reports analysis of more than 55 million sales activities and 5.7 million inbound leads across 400+ companies.
  - It reports stronger conversion when first contact occurs in the first five minutes. The evidence is not specific to forestry-mulching work and should support measurement discipline, not a promised business outcome.
