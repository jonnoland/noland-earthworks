## Day-Rate Agreement Terms

This authorization covers the approved operating time and the defined work area listed in the quote. Billable operating time begins when productive work starts, unless the quote separately states a mobilization or arrival charge. Production varies with vegetation density, terrain, access, hidden material, weather, equipment safety, and site conditions. Noland Earthworks may pause or stop work when conditions are unsafe or outside the written scope.

Work stops when the approved operating time or written not-to-exceed amount is reached. Additional work requires written approval before continuing. This work does not include grading, excavation, hauling, or any other service not specifically listed in the quote. A schedule change caused by weather, ground conditions, access, or safety may require rescheduling; no completion date is guaranteed unless expressly stated in writing.

## One-Day Trial Phase Terms

This is a one-day evaluation phase for the defined work area. Its purpose is to verify production, access, vegetation, and site conditions before additional work is authorized. No specific acreage or total project completion is guaranteed for this phase. Noland Earthworks will document the result and provide a recommendation for any next phase. Further work requires separate written approval.

The trial phase is limited to the work area and operating time stated in the quote. Any additional scope, including work beyond that area, requires written approval before continuation. This work does not include grading, excavation, hauling, or any service not specifically listed in the quote.

## Phased Work Terms

Each phase is a separate, defined portion of the project. Approval and payment for the current phase do not authorize future phases. Any later phase will be scheduled only after written approval, and any stated price assumes the site conditions, access, and scope remain materially the same. Work outside the defined phase requires written approval before it begins.

Optional future phases are not included in the amount due for the current phase. Changes in vegetation, terrain, access, hidden material, utility conditions, weather, or requested outcome may require a written amendment before a future phase begins. Noland Earthworks does not provide grading, excavation, hauling, or services not specifically listed in the approved phase.
