# Full Site Audit — Working Notes

## Public Homepage Review — 2026-08-11

- Strong above-the-fold positioning: owner-operated, veteran-owned, tracked mulcher, and clear primary CTA.
- Multiple quote paths are visible: primary quote CTA, free-estimate CTA, service-specific quote links, and a pricing calculator path.
- Core trust signals are surfaced: licensed/insured, owner-operated, rating, project count, geographic coverage, and response time.
- Address service-area checker, FAQ, gallery, reviews, content resources, and AI chat widget are visible on the page.
- Audit follow-up: assess CTA concentration and intent routing, proof quality, form friction, review credibility, mobile interaction, and whether price/discount language is consistent across all paths.

## Quote Request Flow Review — 2026-08-11

- Quote page establishes response expectations, presents phone/text/email alternatives, and explains the on-site-estimate process before form completion.
- Form captures meaningful qualification information: service, county, client type, acreage, address, selected add-ons, optional job-prep details, and narrative project notes.
- The form is comprehensive but asks for multiple details before submission. Follow-up analysis should evaluate whether a staged form or address-first flow could reduce abandonment while preserving useful qualification data.
- Veteran discount is visible on the form. First-time customer discount is not currently surfaced in the visible quote-form content; the audit should assess consistent offer messaging and conditional-discount explanation.
- Potential conversion risk: the authenticated Ops button is present in the public navigation when the owner is logged in, though it is not exposed to normal public visitors.

## Operational Data Snapshot — 2026-08-11

- The current lead dataset is too small to infer seasonality: six leads are recorded from June through August 2026, with no estimated value populated on the lead records.
- Of those recorded leads, source mix is website (3), Facebook (2), and field app (1). The stages include two lost, one won, one negotiating, one contacted, and one new. This is directional only, not a stable funnel benchmark.
- The chat widget has two recorded sessions and one lead creation, which is encouraging but too small for a reliable conversion rate.
- All six configured background agents are enabled with no logged failures. Pricing updates are active (366 total actions). Lead follow-up has produced six actions. Review-request and visit-reminder agents have produced zero actions, indicating either no eligible paid/completed or scheduled jobs, missing qualifying fields, or a workflow adoption gap.
- The latest AI visibility audit (Aug. 6) reports 9 mentions across 15 prompts, 6 positive and 3 neutral, an overall score of 85, share of voice 50, and zero cited responses. The score is useful for direction, but no citations means the next AEO objective is third-party corroboration and measurable external references, not more internal prompts.
