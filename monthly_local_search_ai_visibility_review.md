# Monthly Local Search, Reviews & AI Visibility Review

**Owner:** Jon Noland  
**Time required:** 15 minutes on the first business day of each month  
**System of record for outcomes:** Noland Earthworks native operations dashboard

## 1. Google Business Profile — 4 minutes

| Check | Record | Action threshold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Profile status | Verified, correct website, correct phone, correct service areas | Fix immediately if any public field is wrong |
| Duplicate listings | Search `Noland Earthworks` and `Noland Earthworks, LLC` in Google Maps | Report/resolve any duplicate, wrong coordinate, wrong phone, or wrong website |
| Reviews | New review count, unanswered reviews, requests sent from completed jobs | Reply to genuine reviews; never create, pay for, or condition an incentive on a positive review |
| Profile activity | Calls, website visits, direction requests, photos/posts since last review | Tie useful activity to actual leads/jobs, not vanity totals |

## 2. Google Search Console — 4 minutes

| Check | Record | Action threshold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Performance | Clicks, impressions, CTR, average position for 28 days vs. prior 28 days | Investigate material declines on high-intent service and county pages |
| Pages | Indexed count and newly excluded URLs | Review every new exclusion by URL; preserve intentional redirects and repair unintended errors |
| Sitemap | Submission state and discovered URLs | Investigate sitemap errors or sharp discovery changes |
| Query/page leads | Which service/county pages generated quote requests or calls | Update only pages with factual job proof or a clear customer question to answer |

## 3. Bing Webmaster Tools — 4 minutes

| Check | Record | Action threshold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sitemap | Processing state, errors/warnings, URLs discovered | Investigate errors; let a new submission process before judging performance |
| Search Performance | Searches, clicks, indexed pages, priority queries | Compare against Search Console; look for service/county gaps |
| AI Performance (beta) | Cited pages, grounding queries, topics, intent, citation share | Treat as observation—not a ranking. Capture cited pages and improve only with verified first-party content |
| URL Inspection | One priority service page and one county page | Inspect if a priority page loses visibility or changes substantially |

## 4. Native Operations Dashboard — 3 minutes

| Check | Record | Action threshold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lead source | Lead source, landing page/UTM, fit decision | Every new record must carry a source and an owner decision |
| Sales handoff | Site visit, proposal, deposit, final-payment status | Any open record without a next action and due date is today's priority |
| Proof | Completed job with consented photos, project facts, and review eligibility | Convert one factual job story into website/social material when approved |
| Attribution | Qualified leads and won jobs by source | Do not fund content, ads, or AI features based on impressions alone |

## Monthly Decision Log

Record only decisions that change work for the next month:

1. **Keep:** Source/pages/workflows producing qualified calls, site visits, approved quotes, deposits, or paid jobs.
2. **Fix:** One technical, profile, or follow-up issue with an owner and due date.
3. **Publish:** One evidence-backed project story or decision guide drawn from an actual job.
4. **Stop:** Any tactic that has no attributable qualified outcome after a fair review period.

> Do not use an aggregate “AI visibility score” as a revenue or ranking metric. Use verified Search Console, Google Business Profile, Bing, and native lead-source data together.
